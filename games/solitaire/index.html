♠️ Play Solitaire Online Free

The classic single-player card game. Sort all cards by suit and rank.

How to play: Click a card to select it, then click where it should go. Build tableau piles down in alternating colours (K→Q→J…), and build each foundation up by suit from A to K. Click the stock (blue back) to draw a card. Double-click a card to send it to its foundation when possible.

Simplified Solitaire

Simplified Klondike: click-to-move instead of drag-and-drop, draw-1 stock with unlimited redeals, and cards cannot be moved back out of a foundation. The full Klondike rules for building tableau and foundations still apply.

Moves: 0
Cards in foundations: 0 / 52
Stock: 0
Click the stock to draw, or click a face-up card to select it.

Tableau: descending, alternating red/black. Empty tableau pile: only a King (or a stack starting with a King). Foundation: same suit, ascending from Ace.
