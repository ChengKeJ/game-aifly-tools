🗺️ Play Europe at War Online Free

Command your nation in this turn-based strategy simulation. Manage your economy, research technology, and conquer Europe. Play this game online for free on desktop and mobile.

How to play: Claim all regions, ally the continent, or build twelve factories to win. Select a nation, declare war through the diplomacy menu, and move troops region by region. Each attack or movement drains energy by one point—manage stamina or stall your blitz. Recruit, Research, Conquer Gold flows from controlled regions and factories. Spend it on barracks for infantry, airfields for bombers, or factories for income. Research trees unlock military, economic, and diplomatic upgrades. Each month you earn gold and choose your next move—stack troops on borders, fortify chokepoints, or launch airborne strikes behind enemy lines. Troops with zero energy sit idle until next turn.
