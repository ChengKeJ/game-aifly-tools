Transform black-and-white images into vibrant art by following numbered instructions. This relaxing color game is free to play online on desktop, iOS, and Android.

How to play: The goal is to transform black-and-white images into vibrant works of art by coloring them according to numbered areas. Start by selecting a category from the gallery, then pick a picture to begin. From there, choose a color from the palette at the bottom of the screen, locate the matching numbered areas in the image, and fill them in to bring the picture to life. Zoom controls let you focus on fine details — adjust the slider at the top of the screen, use pinch gestures on touch devices, or scroll with your mouse wheel. When you want to move faster through a coloring, boosters are available as special tools to speed up the process and help achieve the best results.
