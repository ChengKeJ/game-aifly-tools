Combine identical fruits to create larger varieties in this matching puzzle. Play this game for free online on desktop, Android, and iOS devices.

How to play: Click Play below. The game runs in your browser, no download or sign-up. Fruits fall randomly into the container. When two identical fruits meet, they merge into the next larger fruit on the chain. The mission is to keep pairing matching fruits to build the biggest possible fruit while preventing the container from overflowing past its upper boundary line. The more fruits accumulate, the harder it becomes to find matching pairs, so thoughtful placement matters from the very first drop. Earning points gradually collects illustrations from the fruit-garden story. Controls: Desktop — Click : drop and place fruits Mobile — Tap : drop and place fruits
