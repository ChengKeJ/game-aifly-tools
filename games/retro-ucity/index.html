🎮 Play µCity Online Free

µCity is a tiny city-building simulation for the Game Boy Color. Zone residential, commercial and industrial districts, lay down power lines and roads, and watch your little city grow — a SimCity-style game that fits in 128 KB.

How to play: Click the Start button in the player below. Arrow keys move the cursor, X selects and builds, Z cancels. Use the in-game menu to zone districts and build power plants and roads. Fullscreen is available from the player menu. No download, no sign-up. On mobile, use the on-screen gamepad.

Rotate your phone for a bigger view

Keyboard: Arrow keys = D-pad, X = A button, Z = B button, Enter = Start, Shift = Select. The emulator menu (top-right) offers save states, fullscreen and controls.

About µCity

µCity (v1.3) by AntonioND is an open-source city builder for the Game Boy Color, licensed under the GPL-3.0. Zone land, provide power, keep your citizens happy and grow your city from a village into a metropolis. It runs here in your browser with the Gambatte emulator core via EmulatorJS.
