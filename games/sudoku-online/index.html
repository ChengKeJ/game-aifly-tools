🔢 Play Sudoku Online Online Free

Challenge your logic with Sudoku Online. This free game is playable online on desktop, Android, and iOS. Master classic grids or try X-Sudoku, Windoku, and more.

How to play: Click Play below. The game runs in your browser, no download or sign-up. Fill a 9×9 grid with digits 1 through 9 so each digit appears exactly once in every row, every column, and every 3×3 box. Pre-filled cells are your starting clues. Start With the Most Constrained Cells Look first at cells with the most filled neighbors — fewer remaining options make the correct digit easier to identify. Each cell filled narrows the choices for adjacent ones, and the puzzle resolves through a chain of logical steps. Every puzzle has exactly one solution reachable through pure logic, no guessing required. Use Notes Mode Write multiple digit candidates into a cell and eliminate them as the grid fills in. Controls: Click/Tap cell : select a cell Click/Tap digit : enter a digit into the selected cell Notes mode : write candidate digits into a cell
