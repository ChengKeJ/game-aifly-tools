♥️ Play Hearts Card Game Online Free

A classic trick-taking card game. Avoid hearts and the Queen of Spades.

How to play: This is Simplified Hearts — you play against 3 computer opponents with a full 52-card deck. The player with the 2 of Clubs leads the first trick. You must follow suit if you can. Hearts cannot be led until a heart has been played ("broken"). Each heart taken is 1 point, the Queen of Spades is 13 points. Lowest points after all 13 tricks wins the round. Simplified: no card-passing phase, one deal = one round (no multi-round match to 100), and taking all 26 points ("shooting the moon") scores 0 for you and 26 for each opponent.
