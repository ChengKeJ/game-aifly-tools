About Cut the Watermelon into 8 Equal Slices

A one-action slicing game: drag 4 straight cuts across the watermelon and see how equal your 8 slices turn out. Four cuts through the middle at the right angles make perfect eighths — anything else, and the pieces fight it out. Share your score and challenge a friend to beat it.
