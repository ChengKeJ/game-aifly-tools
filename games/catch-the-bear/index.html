Solve logic puzzles by sliding blocks to match bears by color. This cozy puzzle game is free to play online on desktop, iOS, and Android devices.

How to play: Easy to start, tricky to master. The first few levels teach you the ropes, then obstacles and tighter spaces show up fast. Controls Built for mobile but runs smooth on desktop. Desktop: Click and drag blocks from the queue to the grid. Plan your route before you drop. Mobile: Tap and slide. Responsive touch controls—no lag. Placing Your Bears You start with a queue of colored bear blocks—L-shapes, I-shapes, you name it. Slide them onto the 8x8 dirt grid. Match each bear to its colored hole.
