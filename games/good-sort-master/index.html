🧩 Play Good Sort Master: Triple Match Online Free

Organize items on shelves by matching three identical objects to clear levels. This relaxing game is free to play online on desktop, iOS, and Android devices.

How to play: Easy to grasp. Hard to stop. Responsive across all devices. You start with a wooden shelf grid packed with random groceries. Scan for duplicates—say, three tomatoes scattered across different slots. Drag one tomato to a compartment. Then drag the other two into the same space. Boom. They vanish. The shelf breathes. The real puzzle kicks in when shelves are dense. You might see milk cartons blocking cheese wedges. Move the milk first to free up the cheese. But wait—if you stack milk carelessly, you'll trap juice boxes. Every drag matters. Every choice creates ripples. It's a zen game of spatial foresight. Remove all items on the shelves to beat a level. Early stages use simple H-shaped layouts with 8-12 items. Later? You're juggling 5x4 grids crammed with 20+ objects. The deeper you go, the more you discover: banana-juice boxes, wine bottles, canned goods. Completing levels unlocks fresh item sets—your reward for mastering the sort.
