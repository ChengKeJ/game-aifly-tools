🎮 Play Retroid Online Free

Retroid is a Breakout / Arkanoid clone for the Nintendo Game Boy, originally released in 2016. Bounce the ball, break the bricks, grab power-ups and fight through 20 stages to the boss battle.

How to play: Click the Start button in the player below. Arrow keys move the paddle, X launches the ball. Catch falling power-ups — and watch out for the malus item. Fullscreen is available from the player menu. No download, no sign-up. On mobile, use the on-screen gamepad.

Keyboard: Arrow keys = D-pad, X = A button, Z = B button, Enter = Start, Shift = Select. The emulator menu (top-right) offers save states, fullscreen and controls.

About Retroid

Retroid (2016) by Jonas Fischbach is a free homebrew Breakout clone for the Game Boy, with 20 stages, 5 stage designs, 6 power-up items (plus 1 malus item), 8 chiptunes, a boss battle and an ending sequence. It runs here in your browser with the Gambatte emulator core via EmulatorJS.
