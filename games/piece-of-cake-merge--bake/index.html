🍰 Play Piece of Cake: Merge & Bake Online Free

Restore your grandfather's café in this merge puzzle game. Combine ingredients, unlock dishes, and renovate 21 locations. Playable online and free on mobile or desktop.

How to play: Start with an abandoned café and restore it step by step by earning money through cooking. Combine ingredients on the table to unlock increasingly complex dishes, and merge items to expand your territory so the table can hold more ingredients at once. Upgrade kitchen tools to cook more efficiently, and use the money earned to repair the café and other areas. New locations unlock as you progress, each requiring restoration in turn. From the first missions, the game guides you through what to do, and hints appear whenever you might miss something.
