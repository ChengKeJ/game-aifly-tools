Solve thread-sorting puzzles by organizing colorful yarns on your desktop, iOS, or Android device. This online game is free to play and offers a relaxing challenge.

How to play: Collect colorful threads from various knitted objects and place them into matching colored boxes. The goal is to distribute all threads onto spools without filling the spare slots — clear every spool that way and the level is won. Run out of free slots before fully unraveling the toys, and the level must be restarted. Think carefully before placing threads into temporary slots, because as levels advance, shapes grow more complex, colors multiply, and elements become trickier. Devising a strategy for which spools to target — and accurately identifying shades to avoid wasting free slots — becomes increasingly important.
