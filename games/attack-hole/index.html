🕳️ Play Attack Hole Online Free

Swallow weapons with a black hole before the timer runs out and defeat giant enemies. This arcade shooter is playable online for free on desktop, Android, and iOS.

How to play: The run splits into two distinct phases. During the first phase, move the black hole across the arena and swallow as many weapons as possible before time expires. Every weapon absorbed adds to your firepower. When the timer runs out, the second phase begins: use the full collection of weapons you have gathered to fight and destroy the giant enemy. The outcome of that final battle depends entirely on how much you managed to swallow in the time available.
