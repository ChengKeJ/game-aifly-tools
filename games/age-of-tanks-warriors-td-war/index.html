How to play: Age of Tanks Warriors: TD War is a one-click battle game, so the combat itself is straightforward—the real challenge is deciding how to deploy your army and weapons to eliminate the opposing force before they reach your fortress. Each level tasks you with defeating the enemy army and destroying their shelter. The game opens with your stone cave positioned directly opposite your opponent's cave. Deploying soldiers is done by clicking the small icon at the bottom right of the screen, though the number of soldiers available may not always be enough to match an incoming enemy wave in time. As levels advance, the fortresses grow more formidable, raising the difficulty throughout the ages.
